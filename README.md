# GuildHub – bug reports and ideas

GuildHub is a guild addon for **World of Warcraft: Forever**: who is online and has time, who can craft what, which dungeon quests your guildies share, guild events, craft requests and more.

This repository is only for **bug reports and ideas**. The addon itself is installed with the CurseForge app.

## Report a bug

The easiest way is from inside the game:

1. Open GuildHub (`/gh`), go to **Settings** and click **Report a bug** – or type `/gh bug`.
2. Press **Ctrl+C** to copy the link (it is already selected) and paste it into your browser.
3. Describe what happened and click **Create**.

The link fills in your GuildHub version, the game client version and the last errors GuildHub caught. No character or guild names are included – reports here are public.

You can also click **New issue** above and choose **Bug report**.

You need a free GitHub account to create an issue.

## Ideas

Have an idea that would make guild life easier? Click **New issue** and choose **Idea / feature request**.

## Good to know

- GuildHub never posts anything in guild chat. Addons talk to each other in the hidden addon channel.
- Please write one bug per issue, and check the open issues first – maybe someone has already reported it. A 👍 on an existing issue helps too.
